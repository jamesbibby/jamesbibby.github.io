A Few of My Favourite Things

A 50th Birthday Wine & Cheese Voyage — The Cottage

All six cheese pairings sourced from La Jolie Cheese Shop, our favourite local purveyor.

Pour 1: The Sun-Drenched Sentinel

The Bottle Bastide de la Ciselette Lou Pigna Bandol Rosé 2025 (Provence, France) — A fresh, fruit-forward blend (65% Mourvèdre) with a deceptively pale hue. Bone-dry, with vibrant tart red cherry and berry fruit lifted by racy acidity.
The Cheese Tomme de Chambrille (Poitou-Charentes, France) — A velvety, lactic goat's milk chèvre with bright lemon and meadow grass notes.
The Bite Provençal Tapenade Noire on Crostini — Black olive and caper spread with a dollop of Mariposa Market Sweet Red Pepper Jelly.

Pour 2: The High-Altitude Hero

The Bottle Chartron et Trébuchet Montagny 1er Cru 2023 (Burgundy, France) — A pristine Premier Cru Chardonnay, part barrel-fermented, with white flowers, honeysuckle, citrus, toasted hazelnut and brioche, finishing long and chalky.
The Cheese Jura Flore Comté (Jura, France) — An iconic alpine raw cow's milk cheese, complex, nutty, and slightly caramelized.
The Bite Warm Honeyed Comté Crostini — Toasted baguette brushed with Alpine Wildflower Honey and sea salt.

Pour 3: The Forest Wanderer

The Bottle Butterfield Les Boucherottes Beaune 1er Cru 2011 (Burgundy, France) — A beautifully mature Pinot Noir at its peak: plum, blackcurrant, and tertiary forest-floor earthiness.
The Cheese Grand Crème (Burgundy, France) — An ultra-luxurious double-cream cow's milk cheese from Fromagerie Delin, pure melted butter in texture.
The Bite Homemade Thyme & Wild Mushroom Pâté — Smooth mushroom pâté with garlic and shallots on Raincoast crackers.

Pour 4: The Local Showstopper

The Bottle Stratus Cabernet Franc 'Decant' 2020 (Niagara, Ontario) — An opulent, unfiltered powerhouse bottled on its lees: dense tannins, dark cocoa, and roasted red pepper.
The Cheese 8-Year Aged Perron Cheddar (Quebec, Canada) — An extra-aged, legendary Canadian cheddar, crumbly and sharp with flavor crystals.
The Bite Fire-Kissed Rosemary Lamb Lollipops — Grilled lamb chops with rosemary, brushed with Mariposa Market Red Pepper Jelly glaze.

Pour 5: The King of Wines

The Bottle Azelia Margheria Barolo 2013 (Piedmont, Italy) — A broad-shouldered, legendary Nebbiolo at its peak: cherry liqueur, tobacco leaf, tar, and iron.
The Cheese Testun al Barolo (Piedmont, Italy) — A piquant artisan cheese aged inside the pressed grape must and skins from local Barolo winemaking.
The Bite White Truffle & Sea Salt Fingerling Potatoes — Crispy potatoes with White Truffle Oil from Alba and Parmigiano-Reggiano.

Pour 6: The Golden Treasure

The Bottle Château La Tour Blanche Sauternes 2009 (Bordeaux, France) — A luxurious, unctuous dessert wine from a legendary vintage, earning 95+ scores from critics: rich tropical fruit and clean acidity.
The Cheese Roquefort AOC (France) — The definitive, sharp French sheep's milk blue, the benchmark salt-and-sweet contrast.
The Bite Blue Cheese & Walnut Phyllo Tartlets — Warm pastry cups with toasted walnut and crumbled Roquefort.
Fair winds and following seas — thank you for celebrating with us at The Cottage.
