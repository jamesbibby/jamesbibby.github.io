Interactive Itinerary

Follow the day-by-day flow of your journey. This timeline outlines each major phase, from work commitments in Paris to cultural deep-dives in Alba and Amsterdam.

Oct 4-5: Transatlantic Crossing ✈️

Depart Toronto (YYZ) on LH6825 (operated by Air Canada), arriving at Paris (CDG) on Oct 5. Transfer to accommodation and acclimatize.

Oct 6-11: Paris: Work & Leisure 🇫🇷

Five days of work at the Datadog office, followed by a Saturday dedicated to visiting friends.

Oct 12-16: London Calling 🇬🇧

Travel via Eurostar to London for four full days to connect with friends and explore the city's cultural offerings like the BFI Film Festival or Frieze Art Fair.

Oct 17-20: Piedmont Expedition 🇮🇹

Fly to Turin (TRN), pick up a rental car, and drive to Alba for the International White Truffle Fair. Includes a cooking class on the final morning.

Oct 20-25: Amsterdam Dance Event 🇳🇱

Fly from Turin to Amsterdam. Immerse in the world's largest electronic music festival while staying with friends.

Oct 25-26: Final Leg & Departure ✈️

Take an evening Eurostar from Amsterdam back to Paris. Enjoy a final morning before departing from CDG on AC0873.
