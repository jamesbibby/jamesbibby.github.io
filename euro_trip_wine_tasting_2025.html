EuroTrip 2025

Your Guide to the Tasting

Welcome to our EuroTrip 2025 wine and cheese tasting! This guide is designed to enhance your experience by providing a brief overview of each wine and its suggested pairings. The cheeses for this event have been thoughtfully selected from a local purveyor, La Jolie Cheese Shop.

1. Spain: Albariño from Rías Baixas

The Wine: A vibrant and refreshing white from the cool, coastal region of Galicia. It is known for its crisp acidity, pronounced salinity, and notes of citrus and green apple.

The Cheese Pairing: We'll be enjoying **Ibores Croute Paprika**, a goat's milk cheese from the Extremadura region of Spain. This semi-firm cheese, which has a distinctive, slightly spicy flavor from its paprika rind, provides a complex contrast to the wine's bright and saline character.

2. Germany: Dry Riesling from Pfalz

The Wine: A sophisticated and dry Riesling that showcases profound minerality and elegant citrus and stone fruit notes.

The Cheese Pairing: Our pairing is **Tomme Aux Fleurs**, a cow's milk cheese from Austria with a nutty flavor and a subtle blend of alpine flowers. The high acidity and mineral notes of the Riesling are perfectly suited to cut through the rich, nutty character of this alpine-style cheese, creating a dynamic and refreshing balance.

3. France: Gamay from Beaujolais

The Wine: A serious and structured red from a Beaujolais *Cru* village, offering vibrant red fruit flavors with an earthy undertone and elegant tannins.

The Cheese Pairing: We will pair this wine with **Pont L'Évêque**, a soft, washed-rind cheese from Normandy, France. This cheese has a rich, creamy texture and a pungent aroma that is balanced by the Gamay's fruity and elegant profile, creating a classic and harmonious match.

4. Italy: Montepulciano d'Abruzzo

The Wine: A full-bodied and rustic red from the central Adriatic coast of Italy, known for its bold fruit, soft tannins, and notes of violet and red currant.

The Cheese Pairing: The local pairing is **Caciocavallo di Podolico**, an Italian aged cow's milk cheese. This cheese, which can have notes of dried fruit and herbs, is firm and savory. It's traditionally paired with structured reds, making it a perfect match for the full-bodied and fruit-forward Montepulciano d'Abruzzo.

5. Portugal: Alicante Bouschet from Alentejo

The Wine: A robust and full-bodied red from Portugal's hot, arid Alentejo region, with complex aromas of black fruits, cocoa, and smoke.

The Cheese Pairing: For this bold wine, we have selected a **surprise funky washed rind cheese**. This style of cheese, with its pungent aroma and rich, creamy texture, provides a thrilling contrast to the Alicante Bouschet's powerful fruit and firm tannins. The intensity of the wine allows it to stand up to the cheese's bold character, making for a memorable and challenging pairing to conclude our tasting journey.
