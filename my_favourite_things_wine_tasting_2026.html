My Favourite Things

A 50th Birthday Wine & Cheese Journey | The Cottage

Pour 1: The Sun-Drenched Sentinel

The Secret Clue
A legendary estate's treasure poured from a massive double-bottle. Shimmering gemstone hues with a dry, bracingly crisp finish—like a cool breeze lifting off the Mediterranean waves.
The Revealed Bottle

Bastide de la Ciselette Lou Pigna Bandol Rosé 2025 (Provence, France)

A "Grand Cru" style rosé dominated by Mourvèdre, delivering immense bone-dry structure, persistent orange peel aromas, and a gorgeous, mineral-driven salinity.

The Cheese Pairing

Tomme de Chambrille (France): A velvety, lactic French goat's milk chèvre sourced from La Jolie Cheese Shop. It brings bright, vibrant notes of fresh lemon and meadow grass.

The Custom Bite

Provençal Tapenade Noire on Crostini: Rich black olive and caper spread, accented with a single tiny dollop of Mariposa Market Sweet Red Pepper Jelly to masterfully counter the wine's raw marine salinity.

Pour 2: The High-Altitude Hero

The Secret Clue
An elegant white from the mountains, known for its crisp energy, stone fruit focus, and soft whispers of toasted hazelnut. If Burgundy had a "Goldilocks" white, this would be it.
The Revealed Bottle

Chartron et Trébuchet Montagny 1er Cru 2023 (Burgundy, France)

A pristine Premier Cru Chardonnay aged gracefully on fine lees, offering precise balance, honeysuckle aromas, and a long, chalky mineral finish.

The Cheese Pairing

Jura Flore Comté (Jura, France): An iconic alpine raw cow's milk cheese from Burgundy's mountain neighbor, boasting complex, nutty, and slightly caramelized flavors.

The Custom Bite

Warm Honeyed Comté Crostini: Toasted baguette rounds brushed with rich Alpine Wildflower Honey and coarse sea salt, designed to draw out the wine's delicate brioche and oak tones.

Pour 3: The Forest Wanderer

The Secret Clue
A 15-year-old museum treasure crafted by a legendary Toronto native in Beaune. Expect the complex, silky perfume of wild berries intertwined with the unmistakable scent of a damp forest floor.
The Revealed Bottle

Butterfield Les Boucherottes Beaune 1er Cru 2011 (Burgundy, France)

A beautifully mature Pinot Noir at its absolute peak. Time has melted away its youthful tannins, resolving into a fleshy, generous core of plum, blackcurrant, and tertiary forest-floor earthiness.

The Cheese Pairing

Grand Crème (Burgundy, France): An ultra-luxurious, decadent triple-cream cow's milk cheese crafted by Fromagerie Delin in the heart of Burgundy. Pure melted butter in texture.

The Custom Bite

Homemade Thyme & Wild Mushroom Pâté: Smooth, rustic mushroom pâté seasoned with garlic and shallots, served on Raincoast crackers. The deep earthy umami seamlessly meshes with the aged soul of the wine.

Pour 4: The Local Showstopper

The Secret Clue
A rare, muscular monolith from right here in Ontario, presented in an avant-garde, jagged bottle designed to trap its lees. Dense, dark, and highly focused on evergreen and rosemary.
The Revealed Bottle

Stratus Cabernet Franc 'Decant' 2020 (Niagara, Ontario)

An opulent, unfiltered powerhouse from a historic, hot vintage. Bottled on its lees for an unctuous, mouthcoating texture, it boasts dense tannins alongside notes of dark cocoa and roasted red pepper.

The Cheese Pairing

8-Year Aged Perron Cheddar (Quebec, Canada): An extra-aged, legendary Canadian cheddar. Crumbly, sharp, and laden with intense flavor crystals that effortlessly anchor the wine's massive 14.9% alcohol.

The Custom Bite

Fire-Kissed Rosemary Lamb Lollipops: Small lamb chops marinated in heavy rosemary, flashed on the cottage grill, and brushed with a caramelized Mariposa Market Red Pepper Jelly glaze.

Pour 5: The King of Wines

The Secret Clue
The ultimate dry red splurge from a classic, 5-star vintage. Nicknamed "the King of wines," this traditional bottle demands a massive decant just to slowly unmask its royal face.
The Revealed Bottle

Azelia Margheria Barolo 2013 (Piedmont, Italy)

A broad-shouldered, legendary Nebbiolo entering its ideal peak drinking window. It features robust, muscular structure laced with complex notes of cherry liqueur, tobacco leaf, tar, and iron.

The Cheese Pairing

Testun al Barolo (Piedmont, Italy): A piquant, holy-grail artisan cheese aged directly inside the pressed grape must and skins left over from local Barolo winemaking.

The Custom Bite

White Truffle & Sea Salt Fingerling Potatoes: Crispy warm potatoes tossed in authentic, aromatic White Truffle Oil brought back directly from Alba, topped with freshly grated Parmigiano-Reggiano.

Pour 6: The Golden Treasure

The Secret Clue
An amber-colored nectar from my personal library cellar. A highly acclaimed vintage dripping with botrytis complexity, candied ginger, and honeyed apricot.
The Revealed Bottle

Château La Tour Blanche Sauternes 2009 (Bordeaux, France)

A luxurious, unctuous, 99-point dessert masterpiece. Rich, heavily concentrated, and balancing intense tropical fruit sweetness with beautifully clean acidity.

The Cheese Pairing

Roquefort AOC (France): The definitive, sharp, and deeply pungent French sheep's milk blue cheese. The global benchmark for a perfect salt-and-sweet flavor contrast.

The Custom Bite

Blue Cheese & Walnut Phyllo Tartlets: Miniature warm pastry cups filled with toasted walnut, crumbled Roquefort, and a sweet, spicy swirl of Mariposa Market Red Pepper Jelly.

No wine or cheese expertise required—just a glass, an appetite, and a sense of adventure.
