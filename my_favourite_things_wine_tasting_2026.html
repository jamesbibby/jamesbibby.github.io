A Few of My Favourite Things

A 50th Birthday Wine & Cheese Voyage | The Cottage
  ✦  

All six cheese pairings were sourced from La Jolie Cheese Shop, our favourite local purveyor.

Pour 1: The Sun-Drenched Sentinel

The Clue
A treasure from a legendary Provence estate. Deceptively pale and almost translucent in the glass, it hides a bone-dry soul built for vibrancy and immediate charm.
The Bottle

Bastide de la Ciselette Lou Pigna Bandol Rosé 2025 (Provence, France)

A fresh, fruit-forward blend (65% Mourvèdre) with a "deceptively pale" hue. Bone-dry on the palate, it delivers vibrant tart red cherry and berry fruit lifted by racy, perfectly balanced acidity.

The Cheese

Tomme de Chambrille (Poitou-Charentes, France): A velvety, lactic French goat's milk chèvre. It brings bright, vibrant notes of fresh lemon and meadow grass.

The Bite

Provençal Tapenade Noire on Crostini: Rich black olive and caper spread, accented with a single tiny dollop of Mariposa Market Sweet Red Pepper Jelly. The briny, savory tapenade acts as a grounded anchor for the wine's fresh, racy acidity.

Pour 2: The High-Altitude Hero

The Clue
An elegant white from the mountains, known for its crisp energy, stone fruit focus, and soft whispers of toasted hazelnut. Not too heavy, not too lean—the "Goldilocks" of whites, just right in every sip.
The Bottle

Chartron et Trébuchet Montagny 1er Cru 2023 (Burgundy, France)

A pristine Premier Cru Chardonnay, part barrel-fermented, showing an expressive nose of white flowers and honeysuckle over citrus and lemon, with soft toasted hazelnut and brioche notes from its time in oak, finishing long, chalky, and mineral.

The Cheese

Jura Flore Comté (Jura, France): An iconic alpine raw cow's milk cheese from Burgundy's mountain neighbor, boasting complex, nutty, and slightly caramelized flavors.

The Bite

Warm Honeyed Comté Crostini: Toasted baguette rounds brushed with rich Alpine Wildflower Honey and coarse sea salt, designed to draw out the wine's delicate brioche and oak tones.

Pour 3: The Forest Wanderer

The Clue
A 15-year-old museum treasure, crafted by a legendary Toronto native in a small, storied French village. Expect the complex, silky perfume of wild berries intertwined with the unmistakable scent of a damp forest floor.
The Bottle

Butterfield Les Boucherottes Beaune 1er Cru 2011 (Burgundy, France)

A beautifully mature Pinot Noir at its absolute peak. Time has melted away its youthful tannins, resolving into a fleshy, generous core of plum, blackcurrant, and tertiary forest-floor earthiness.

The Cheese

Grand Crème (Burgundy, France): An ultra-luxurious, decadent double-cream cow's milk cheese crafted by Fromagerie Delin in the heart of Burgundy. Pure melted butter in texture.

The Bite

Homemade Thyme & Wild Mushroom Pâté: Smooth, rustic mushroom pâté seasoned with garlic and shallots, served on Raincoast crackers. The deep earthy umami seamlessly meshes with the aged soul of the wine.

Pour 4: The Local Showstopper

The Clue
A rare, muscular monolith from right here in Ontario, presented in a striking, jagged bottle built to add extra richness as it ages. Dense, dark, and highly focused on evergreen and rosemary.
The Bottle

Stratus Cabernet Franc 'Decant' 2020 (Niagara, Ontario)

An opulent, unfiltered powerhouse from a historic, hot vintage. Bottled on its lees for an unctuous, mouthcoating texture, it boasts dense tannins alongside notes of dark cocoa and roasted red pepper.

The Cheese

8-Year Aged Perron Cheddar (Quebec, Canada): An extra-aged, legendary Canadian cheddar. Crumbly, sharp, and laden with intense flavor crystals that effortlessly anchor the wine's massive 14.9% alcohol.

The Bite

Fire-Kissed Rosemary Lamb Lollipops: Small lamb chops marinated in heavy rosemary, flashed on the cottage grill, and brushed with a caramelized Mariposa Market Red Pepper Jelly glaze.

Pour 5: The King of Wines

The Clue
The ultimate dry red splurge from a classic, 5-star vintage. Nicknamed "the King of wines," this traditional bottle demands a massive decant just to slowly unmask its royal face.
The Bottle

Azelia Margheria Barolo 2013 (Piedmont, Italy)

A broad-shouldered, legendary Nebbiolo entering its ideal peak drinking window. It features robust, muscular structure laced with complex notes of cherry liqueur, tobacco leaf, tar, and iron.

The Cheese

Testun al Barolo (Piedmont, Italy): A piquant, holy-grail artisan cheese aged directly inside the pressed grape must and skins left over from local Barolo winemaking.

The Bite

White Truffle & Sea Salt Fingerling Potatoes: Crispy warm potatoes tossed in authentic, aromatic White Truffle Oil brought back directly from Alba, topped with freshly grated Parmigiano-Reggiano.

Pour 6: The Golden Treasure

The Clue
An amber-colored nectar I've been saving for a while. A highly acclaimed vintage, made from grapes left on the vine until they shriveled into liquid gold, dripping with candied ginger and honeyed apricot.
The Bottle

Château La Tour Blanche Sauternes 2009 (Bordeaux, France)

A luxurious, unctuous dessert masterpiece from a legendary vintage, earning 95+ scores from critics. Rich, heavily concentrated, and balancing intense tropical fruit sweetness with beautifully clean acidity.

The Cheese

Roquefort AOC (France): The definitive, sharp, and deeply pungent French sheep's milk blue cheese, served lightly crumbled over the tartlets for the ultimate salt-and-sweet contrast.

The Bite

Apricot & Walnut Phyllo Tartlets: Miniature warm pastry cups filled with toasted walnuts and chopped dried apricot, mirroring the wine's own honeyed apricot and candied ginger.

No wine or cheese expertise required—just a glass, an appetite, and a sense of adventure.
